AQACT

Air Quality Analysis & Compliance Assessment Tool

Purpose

AQACT helps researchers, environmental officers, policy makers, and journalists turn sensor data into understandable air-quality evidence. It supports data cleaning, exploration, visualization, compliance screening, and model-based analysis.

Typical Workflow
1. Upload CSV or Excel sensor data.
2. Map source columns to canonical air-quality fields.
3. Clean, validate, and explore the readings.
4. Review plots, compliance episodes, and model outputs.
5. Export evidence for reporting and further review.
Analysis Scope
Data
Provider cleaning, column mapping, missing values, resampling, and export.
Compliance
WHO and Country's EPA guideline comparisons, episodes, timelines, and response guidance.
Models
Trend, seasonal baseline, anomaly detection, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, and related models.
Evidence
Charts, summary statistics, extreme events, maps, and model evaluation metrics.
Responsible Use

Compliance results are screening evidence, not a legal determination. Confirm the applicable national standard, units, averaging period, instrument calibration, siting requirements, and data completeness with the responsible authority before enforcement, public alerts, or publication.

Model Evaluation

Pair an observed column against a model column and compute MB, RMSE, R, IOA, FAC2, KGE and more.
